Delete your data

Bucket Bloom: Bead Sort Puzzle · by IronClad Creation

We, the developer, do not run our own servers or keep player accounts. Your data lives on your phone, in your own Google account, and with our ad partner Unity. Here is how to delete each part.

1. Ask us (we handle it for you)

  • Email twothumbsgamingyt@gmail.com with the subject "Bucket Bloom data deletion".
  • If you can, include your Google Play Games name (or the email of your Google account) so we can help with the right data.
  • We reply within 30 days, explain what was deleted, and forward the request to Google and Unity where needed.

2. Do it yourself right away

  • Progress on your phone (levels, stars, items, settings): Android Settings → Apps → Bucket Bloom → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  • Google Play Games data (leaderboard scores, achievements, cloud save): open the Google Play Games app → Profile → Settings → delete the data for Bucket Bloom (or your whole Play Games profile).
  • Advertising data (Unity Ads): delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. You can also make a request to Unity through the Unity privacy policy.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: game progress, items, settings, leaderboard scores, achievements and cloud save for Bucket Bloom.
  • Kept by Google: records of purchases you made through Google Play (Google keeps these for legal and tax reasons, under Google's policy). Remove ads can still be restored if you reinstall.
  • Unity: advertising data is kept and deleted according to Unity's own retention rules.
