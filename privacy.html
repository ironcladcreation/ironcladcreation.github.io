Privacy Policy

Bucket Bloom: Bead Sort Puzzle · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Bucket Bloom: Bead Sort Puzzle ("the game") is made by IronClad Creation ("we", "us"), an independent developer in South Africa. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

1. What stays on your phone

Your game progress (levels, stars, rewards, items such as Reveal pots and Belt slots) and your settings (sound, vibration) are saved on your device. We, the developer, do not run our own servers and do not receive this information.

2. Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the game uses Google Play Games Services for:

  • Leaderboards – your Play Games name, avatar and the scores the game submits (stars today, this week and in total). Depending on your Play Games privacy settings, other players can see these on the leaderboards.
  • Friends – your Play Games friends list, only if you allow the game to see it.
  • Achievements – which achievements you have unlocked.
  • Cloud save – a copy of your game progress stored in your own Google account ("Saved Games"), so you can continue on a new phone or after reinstalling.

This information is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in. You can delete your Play Games data for this game in the Google Play Games app (Profile → Settings).

3. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game is free and shows ads through Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): optional reward videos that you choose to watch, and occasional short, skippable ads between levels. To show and measure ads, Unity collects information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset it or delete it in your phone's settings),
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • how you interact with ads (for example views, taps and completed videos).

Unity uses this to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show ads that match your interests. Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your choices: players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent pop-up (by Google) the first time they open the game, and can change their choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices in the game. On any Android phone you can turn off ad personalisation by deleting your advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads).

Buying Remove ads turns off the ads between levels. Optional reward videos stay available if you want them.

4. In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases (Remove ads and packs of Reveal pots and Belt slots). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was made, so it can deliver what you bought and restore Remove ads if you reinstall. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

5. Sharing

If you tap a share button, the game opens the app you pick (for example WhatsApp) with a message and a picture of your progress. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

6. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's POPIA or the EU/UK GDPR) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to its use. Because we do not hold your data ourselves, most requests are best made directly with Google (Play Games, Play purchases) or Unity (ads) via the links above. We are happy to help: just contact us.

8. Security and retention

Progress saved on your phone stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data handled by Google and Unity is kept according to their own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
